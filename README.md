## Four ways to pay without a credit card

| Option | How it works | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|
| App Store / Google Play | Subscribe inside the ChatGPT app and pay with your store balance (for example a store gift card) | You need a store account in a region where the app and that payment work; pricing follows the local store; cancel and refund through the store |
| Official ChatGPT gift card | Redeem a PIN at chatgpt.com/redeem | As of our 2026-10-24 check, sold and usable in the US only; see [gift-cards.md](gift-cards.md) |
| Top-up service ("daichong") | A seller pays; the plan is activated on your own account | Not an official channel; no refunds from OpenAI; pick a seller that never asks for your password |
| Shared / pre-made accounts | You log into someone else's account | **Not recommended**: others can read your chats, and shared logins are a common ban trigger |

Also check the region: OpenAI publishes a [list of supported countries and territories](https://platform.openai.com/docs/supported-countries) and says accounts used from unsupported regions may be suspended. Mainland China, Hong Kong and Macau are not on that list (checked 2026-10-26). This guide does not explain how to get around region or payment restrictions.

## Is "cheap ChatGPT Plus" real? What to check

Before paying any seller, check these six points:

1. **Is the price plausible?** Offers far below $20 per month are often refund-abuse or loophole orders that get revoked days later.
2. **Do they ask for your password or a verification code?** If yes, walk away.
3. **Is the subscription on your own account?** Not a shared or pre-made account.
4. **What happens if activation fails?** Refund or retry should be written down.
5. **What happens if the plan drops mid-month?**
6. **Can you reach a real person after the sale?**
