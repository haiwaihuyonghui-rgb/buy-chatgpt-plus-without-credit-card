## Check your region first

OpenAI publishes a [list of supported countries and territories](https://platform.openai.com/docs/supported-countries) and says accounts accessed from unsupported regions may be suspended. Mainland China, Hong Kong and Macau are not on that list; Taiwan is (checked 2026-10-26). This applies whichever payment route you choose. This guide does not explain how to get around region or payment restrictions.

## Option 1: subscribe on chatgpt.com

- **How:** profile icon → Upgrade Plan → Get Plus.
- **Requirement:** the pricing page FAQ says you can buy Go, Plus, Pro or Business with "any major credit card" ([pricing page](https://openai.com/chatgpt/pricing/), checked 2026-10-26).
- **Pros:** official channel, official support and refunds (refunds typically processed within 5–10 business days after approval, per the Plus help article).
- **Cons:** no card, no subscription; some cards issued outside supported regions are declined.

## Option 2: App Store / Google Play in-app purchase

- **How:** upgrade inside the ChatGPT app and pay with your store payment method or balance (for example an Apple Gift Card redeemed to your Apple Account).
- **Requirements:** a store account where the app and that payment method work. Apple notes gift cards "aren't available in all countries or regions" and some purchases may require a credit card on file even when paying from balance ([Apple Support](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118242), checked 2026-10-26).
- **Pros:** no credit card needed if you have store balance.
- **Cons / risks:** the store, not OpenAI, manages billing; cancel and refund through the store. Subscribing on both the web and in the app leads to double charges ([OpenAI](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001043-how-do-i-avoid-being-charged-twice-if-i-subscribe-to-chatgpt-on-ios-android-and-the-web), checked 2026-10-26). In-app prices follow the local store.

## Option 3: gift cards

- **Official ChatGPT gift card** ([Buying and redeeming OpenAI gift cards](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001491-buying-and-redeeming-openai-gift-cards), checked 2026-10-26): USD cards from participating US retailers, usually $15–$250; available and redeemable **only in the US** for US-based accounts billed in USD; redeemed at chatgpt.com/redeem on the web; usable for subscriptions, renewals and credits on ChatGPT web, **not** for App Store / Google Play purchases; non-refundable once redeemed.
- **Claude gift subscriptions:** currently unavailable to purchase for all accounts ([Anthropic](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12938627-how-to-gift-a-claude-subscription), checked 2026-10-26).
- Details and red flags: [gift-cards.md](gift-cards.md).

## Option 4: top-up service ("daichong")

- **How:** a seller pays and the plan is activated on your own account.
- **Pros:** pay with Alipay / WeChat Pay; your card is never attached, so no auto-renewal.
- **Cons:** not an official channel; OpenAI cannot refund you; after-sales depends entirely on the seller.
- **Risks:** unreliable sellers, revoked "loophole" orders, and account theft if a seller asks for your password.

**Not recommended:** shared or pre-made accounts. Others can read your chats, and shared logins are a common ban trigger.

## Is "cheap ChatGPT Plus" real? Six checks

1. **Is the price plausible?** Offers far below $20 per month are often refund-abuse or loophole orders that get revoked days later.
2. **Do they ask for your password or a verification code?** If yes, walk away.
3. **Is the subscription on your own account?** Not a shared or pre-made account.
4. **What happens if activation fails?** Refund or retry should be written down.
5. **What happens if the plan drops mid-month?**
6. **Can you reach a real person after the sale?**

## FAQ

**Can I pay OpenAI directly with Alipay or WeChat Pay?**
The official pricing page lists major credit cards (checked 2026-10-26). Alipay / WeChat Pay works only through a top-up seller, or indirectly via store balance.

**Is a top-up shop an official OpenAI reseller?**
No. If that matters to you, subscribe officially.

**Can I ask OpenAI for a refund on a top-up order?**
No. You did not pay OpenAI directly, so after-sales issues go to the seller.
