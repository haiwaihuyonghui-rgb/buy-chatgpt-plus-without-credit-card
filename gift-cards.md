## Three things people call a "gift card"

| What you are actually buying | What it does | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Official ChatGPT gift card | Balance on a US ChatGPT account | Usually unusable for accounts outside the US |
| App Store / Google Play gift card | Store balance; subscribe inside the app | Needs a store account in the right region; price follows the local store |
| A seller's card code ("kami") | The seller activates the plan on your account | Not an official gift card; choose a seller with clear refund and warranty rules |

## Four red flags

1. **Far below face value.** Deeply discounted gift cards are often bought with stolen cards; the balance can be frozen after redemption.
2. **Code sent as a blurry image, or only after extra payments.** High risk of a used or fake code.
3. **Wrong region.** A US card only works on a US account.
4. **Mixed labels.** Some sellers call card codes, gift cards and shared accounts all "gift cards". Ask what you are getting before paying.
